Final Product Outcome

When a recruiter lands on your site, they should immediately understand:

You build full-stack applications.
You understand software architecture.
You have experience with AI/ML systems.
You can work across web, mobile, backend, and databases.
You have completed multiple substantial projects beyond classroom exercises.

The site should feel less like "a student portfolio" and more like "the public dashboard of an early-career software engineer." That positioning will do far more for your opportunities than flashy animations or trendy effects.
